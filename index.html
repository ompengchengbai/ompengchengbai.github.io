I am a PhD student in Operations Management at the Lee Kong Chian School of Business, Singapore Management University.

My research interests include algorithmic pricing, learning in dynamic markets, market design, and socially responsible operations.

Working Paper

The Regularization and Optimal Design of All-Pay Auctions [ssrn]

with Bo Chen and Jingfeng Lu

-- develops a regularization procedure that yields unique closed-form equilibria in two-bidder all-pay auctions and characterizes optimal joint favoritism and information disclosure.

Work in Progress

Serving an Aging Society: Targeted or Universal Subsidies for Community Food Centers?

with Xin Fang, Guiyun Feng, and Sergei Savin

-- compares targeted and universal meal subsidies and shows how subsidy scope shapes service expansion, price-quality terms, and senior welfare.

Algorithmic Pricing Competition under Nonstationary Demand

with Ömer Sarıtaç and Arnoud van den Boer

Convex-Order Bounds and Joint Design in Two-Player Contests

-- establishes a sharp convex-order bound for two-player homogeneous contests and uses it to characterize optimal joint information and favoritism design.

Biased All-pay Auctions

-- shows that information disclosure is revenue-neutral under linear bid costs in biased all-pay auctions, while nonlinear costs can overturn this neutrality.

Black-Box Support on Platforms: Demand Mislearning, Operational Distortion, and Value of Transparency

-- studies how opaque temporary platform traffic support can distort demand learning and operational decisions, and quantifies the value of transparency.
