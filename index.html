Pengcheng Bai

I am a third-year PhD student in Operations Management at the Lee Kong Chian School of Business, Singapore Management University. I received my MSc degree from Nanjing University.

More broadly, my research aims to use carefully chosen models to isolate economically meaningful mechanisms and deepen our understanding of real-world phenomena.

My research interests include market design, information design, socially responsible operations, and learning in dynamic markets.
